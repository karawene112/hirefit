Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What we collect

Account: your email address. Usage: the date, match score and headline of each scan, and your remaining credits. Payments: handled by Lemon Squeezy; we receive order and subscription details (no card numbers).

Your CV and job descriptions

Your CV text and job descriptions are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) only to generate your report. HireFit does not store the full text of your CV, job description or generated report on its servers. PDF and Word files are read in your browser; the files themselves are never uploaded. A draft of your text may be kept in your own browser's local storage while you sign in.

Service providers

Supabase (accounts and database), Anthropic (AI analysis), Lemon Squeezy (payments) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each processes data only to provide its service.

Your rights

You can ask us to export or delete your account and data at any time by emailing us.

Cookies

We use only the storage needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.
