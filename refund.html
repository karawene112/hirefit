Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Scan packs

If you have not used any scans from a pack, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. If something went wrong with a scan (for example, the analysis failed), a scan is not deducted, and if a report was clearly broken we will add the scan back on request.

Unlimited subscription

You can cancel anytime from the link in your receipt email; you keep access until the end of the current billing period. If you were charged for a renewal you did not intend and have not used the service since, contact us within 7 days for a refund.

How to request

Email us from the address you used to purchase, with your order number. Refunds are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record, to your original payment method.
